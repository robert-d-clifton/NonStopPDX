Three towers for the model

Drawing the PDX control tower

What the photo shows, and what the model needs to keep:

  • A thick concrete shaft that widens steadily toward the top, about half the cab's width at the base.
  • A pale platform under the cab, the dark glass cab with white frames, and a white cap with antennas.
  • The model draws everything as flat sided blocks with one lit side and one shaded side, so each option uses those same blocks and the terminal's own glass colors.
