Reworking Old Town

Old Town stands in for Amsterdam, Frankfurt, Guadalajara, London and New Orleans. Three directions, drawn with the motif editor's own shapes and colors, each shown large and at the size it appears on a destination page.

A way to use them

One drawing can't really be all five cities. A split works better:

  • Rooftops becomes the shared Old Town motif, for any historic city without its own drawing.
  • Canal row goes to Amsterdam, as a new shared motif or as its custom drawing.
  • Bell towers goes to Guadalajara, and could suit New Orleans too.
  • London and Frankfurt keep Rooftops for now, and get their own custom drawings later if you want: the Elizabeth Tower, the half timbered Römer.

Whichever you pick, I can load it into the motif editor so you can refine it shape by shape.
